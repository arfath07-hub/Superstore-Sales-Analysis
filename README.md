# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Superstore Sales Analysis** project is an end-to-end data analytics project that analyzes retail sales data to uncover business insights, evaluate product performance, identify sales trends, and predict future sales using Machine Learning.

The project demonstrates the complete data analytics workflow, including **data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), SQL analysis, Power BI dashboard development, and predictive modeling** using Python.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate thousands of transactions every day. Understanding customer purchasing behavior, product performance, regional sales, and profitability is essential for making informed business decisions.

The objective of this project is to analyze historical sales data, identify the key drivers of sales, and build a predictive model that supports data-driven decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

* Perform data cleaning and preprocessing.
* Analyze sales, profit, quantity, and discounts.
* Identify top-performing products and categories.
* Analyze regional and customer segment performance.
* Perform SQL-based business analysis.
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
* Predict sales using Linear Regression.
* Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

* Sales vary significantly across orders, with a few high-value transactions contributing a large share of total revenue.
* Higher quantities generally lead to higher sales, making **Quantity** an important predictor of sales.
* Discounts do not always increase sales; excessive discounts can reduce profitability.
* Product categories and sub-categories contribute differently to overall sales, highlighting high-performing product groups.
* Regional sales performance varies, indicating opportunities to improve marketing and inventory planning in lower-performing regions.

---

# 📌 Business Recommendations

* Use discounts strategically to maximize profitability.
* Increase inventory for high-performing products.
* Focus marketing campaigns on low-performing regions.
* Promote products with consistently high profit margins.
* Monitor sales trends to improve forecasting and inventory planning.
